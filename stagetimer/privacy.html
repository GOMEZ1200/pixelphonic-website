Stage Timer

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Stage Timer does not collect, store or share any personal data. Pixel Phonic Ltd receives nothing from the app.

What stays on your Mac

Your timers, running order, messages, settings and imported fonts are saved only on your Mac, inside the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Your local network

Stage Timer runs a small timer service on your Mac so stage screens, phones, Stream Deck and Companion can show and control the timer. Unless you turn on Allow local network access, only your own Mac can reach it. When you turn it on, devices on the same network can connect, and only with an access code or a paired device. Paired devices are remembered on your Mac by name, and you can remove them at any time in Settings → Remote Control.

No tracking

There are no accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking, and the app doesn't use the internet.
