Stage Timer

A countdown timer for live events, made by Pixel Phonic for real shows.

Run timers from your Mac, put them on any stage screen, send messages to the presenter, and control it all from a phone, Stream Deck or Bitfocus Companion.

What it does

  • Countdown, count-up and time-of-day, with amber and red warnings and clear overtime.
  • A running order you can import from a PDF.
  • Stage screens on any display, plus browser viewers on the same network.
  • Presenter messages, flash and blackout.
  • Control from a paired phone, a Stream Deck or Companion.
  • No internet needed. Everything stays on your Mac and local network.
